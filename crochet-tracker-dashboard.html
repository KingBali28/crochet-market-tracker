Crochet Market Tracker

A self-healing scraper that turns Etsy's crochet listings into structured market signal — pricing, demand, and quality data for BaliCreates and a future crochet marketplace.

the problem

Small makers can't see their own market

Crochet sellers have no easy way to see what's actually selling, at what price, and what buyers care about — the tools built for big e-commerce don't serve niche craft markets.

This scraper pulls live Etsy listings and extracts structured fields — title, price, shop, favorites, ratings — as a foundation for pricing guidance and trend spotting.

known limitations

What the data can't tell you yet

🪡 review_count mirrors rating

Every rated listing has review_count exactly equal to rating (e.g. 4.9 / 4.9) — it's not counting reviews. Likely a field-mapping bug in the v2 schema, flagged for a fix.

🪡 Mixed currencies, uncorrected

v2 pulled 15 EUR listings and 9 USD listings with no FX conversion — averaging price across the set without splitting by currency overstates or understates the real picture.

🪡 Sparse favorites & ratings

Only 16 of 24 v1 rows and 22 of 24 v2 rows have a favorites count; just 15 of 24 v2 rows have a rating at all — missing, not zero.

🪡 Single keyword, one search page

Everything comes from one "crochet" search, so v1 and v2 sampled largely different listings (4 overlap) rather than tracking the same items over time.

next steps

Where this goes

Schedule recurring runs against the same listing IDs to track real price and favorites trends over time, rather than resampling search results.

Expand past the single "crochet" keyword into category tracking — bags, amigurumi, cardigans — and fix the review_count field before it feeds seller-facing pricing guidance.
